# Super Admin Guide

## Access
Super Admin users manage the entire platform at `/super-admin`.

## Dashboard
Live metrics: organisations, subscriptions, payments, support tickets and platform activity.

## Organisations
Create, suspend and manage tenant organisations. View branches, subscriptions and owners.

## Plans & billing prep
Manage plan tiers and features. Stripe webhook endpoint is prepared; live billing deferred to v1.1.

## Support tickets
Review and respond to customer tickets.

## CMS & marketing
Manage testimonials, FAQs and marketing content where enabled.

## Email queue
Monitor outbound email status from the Email Queue page.

## Security
Review access requests, audit logs and platform-wide settings.

## Best practices
- Verify organisation before manual plan changes
- Use activity logs for audit trail
- Keep Super Admin accounts MFA-protected
