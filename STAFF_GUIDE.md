# Staff Guide

## Access
Sign in with email and password provided by your manager. Use the Staff panel at `/staff`.

## Install as app (PWA)
On mobile Chrome/Safari: **Add to Home Screen**. The Staff panel works offline for queued actions and syncs when back online.

## Daily tasks

| Task | Where |
|------|-------|
| Clock in/out | Clock |
| Cash up | Cash Up |
| View shifts | Shift |
| Request leave | Leave |
| Shift swap | Shift Swap |
| View hours | Hours |

## Clock-in
Tap Clock and use your PIN or account action buttons.

## Cash-up
Complete the step wizard: coins, notes, cards, platforms, deductions. Submit when balanced.

## Notifications
Check the bell for shift approvals, leave updates and manager messages.

## Profile & security
Update contact details from Profile. Enable 2FA and manage sessions from Account Security.

## Offline mode
If connection drops during clock-in or cash-up, actions queue locally and sync automatically when online.
