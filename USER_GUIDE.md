# User Guide

TotalCashPro helps hospitality and retail teams manage daily operations from one place.

## Getting started

1. **Sign up** at the marketing site — 14-day Professional trial starts automatically
2. **Verify your email** from the inbox link
3. **Complete the setup wizard** — business details, branch, VAT, cash drawer, optional staff and supplier
4. **Open your dashboard** and start with Cash Up or Staff

## Key workflows

### Daily cash-up
Record till counts, card totals, platform deductions and expenses. Submitted cash-ups feed finance and reports automatically.

### Staff & attendance
Add staff with PIN codes for clock-in. Review attendance and approve leave from HR.

### Finance
Track income, expenses, bills, supplier payments and payroll. Purchase orders flow through to goods received and supplier invoices.

### Reports
Use the Reports Center for branch comparisons, exports and saved views.

## Roles
